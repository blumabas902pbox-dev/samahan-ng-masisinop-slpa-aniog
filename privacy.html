Privacy Notice

USWAG Data Privacy Notice · Effective November 24, 2026

USWAG (Unified System for Work and Growth) respects your privacy under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). This notice explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how you stay in control.

Information we collect

  • Account details: Your name or username, barangay/home address, 11-digit mobile contact number, system role, and encrypted password.
  • Sign-in providers: Basic profile identifiers if you choose to sign in with Google, Facebook, or SMS verification.
  • Livelihood & contact records: Product listings, stock movements, sales, expenses, capital logs, and direct messages sent through our Contact form.
  • Browser storage: Local storage entries used to keep your session preferences, welcome alert dismissal, legal agreement status, and sent message tracking active on your device.

Why we use your information

  • To create and secure your member, buyer, seller, or SLPA officer account.
  • To record and summarize association livelihood operations for Samahan ng Masisinop SLPA in Barangay Aniog.
  • To respond to inquiries, send SMS/email status updates on direct messages, and prevent spam or unauthorized access.

How we protect and share data

Passwords are stored securely using cryptographic hashing, and access to administrative controls is restricted to authorized SLP Admins. We do not sell or rent your personal data. Necessary technical services (such as Cloudflare hosting, Google/Meta authentication, and Semaphore SMS delivery) process data only to operate the system.

Your rights and account deletion

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to access, correct, object to, or request the deletion of your personal data. You can delete your own contact messages directly on the Contact Us page, or request full account removal by contacting the association or emailing bryanjaylumabas@gmail.com. Note that finalized financial ledger entries may be retained in anonymized form for association audit integrity.

More details & Data Privacy Officer contact

Data retention

Active account data is kept while your account remains open or as needed to support Samahan ng Masisinop SLPA reporting.

Third-party links

Our pages may link to external sites such as DSWD and the National Privacy Commission. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Questions and complaints

For privacy concerns, reach out via our Contact Us page or email bryanjaylumabas@gmail.com. You also have the right to file a concern with the National Privacy Commission (NPC).
