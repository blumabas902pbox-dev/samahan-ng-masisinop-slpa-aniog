National Privacy Commission of the Philippines

Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) & System Compliance


Welcome to Samahan ng Masisinop - USWAG (Unified System for Work and Growth). In compliance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) of the Philippines, we are committed to safeguarding your personal data and upholding your fundamental right to digital privacy.


Section 1: Collection of Personal Information. By creating an account, you explicitly authorize USWAG and the Barangay Aniog Sustainable Livelihood Program Association (SLPA) to collect, process, record, organize, store, and update your personal data (including Full Name, Permanent Address, Contact Number, and System Role).

Section 2: Purpose and Use of Data. Information collected is strictly utilized for community member authentication, coordinating agricultural and livelihood product transactions between buyers and sellers, managing microenterprise inventory records, and broadcasting essential system announcements.

Section 3: Data Security Measures. In accordance with NPC standards and guidelines, appropriate organizational, physical, and technical security protocols are enforced to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Section 4: Rights of the Data Subject. As a registered user, you retain the right to be informed, to access, rectify, or erase your data, and to lodge complaints with the National Privacy Commission for any privacy violations.
